The learning loop

From “I’ve seen this”
to “I can explain this.”

A lesson is a starting point. Understanding comes from connecting the idea, trying it, and checking your reasoning.

  1. 01 / Learn

    Make the concept concrete.

    Read explanations, trace examples, and use diagrams to build a mental model before reaching for a solution.

  2. 02 / Practice

    Put your reasoning to work.

    Move from a worked example to a problem. Use test results to see where your approach holds up.

  3. 03 / Reflect

    Find the next useful question.

    Sign in for assessments and contextual AI tutoring. Revisit the parts you cannot yet explain in your own words.
