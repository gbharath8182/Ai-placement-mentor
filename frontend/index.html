Before you begin

What can I use without signing in?

The curriculum and lesson explanations are public. Saving progress, submitting assessments, AI tutoring, interviews and resume generation require an account.

What happens to my camera and microphone?

Camera video stays in your browser and is not uploaded. When you choose to transcribe, the audio is sent to the speech provider. Confirmed answers and interview feedback are saved to your account; sessions can be deleted in the interview workspace.

Does a score mean I’m ready to be hired?

No. Assessment and interview feedback are practice aids, not hiring predictions or validated measures of ability. Check generated claims and use real interviews and project work as additional evidence.

Are coding and AI tools always available?

Code execution requires a configured remote sandbox. AI tools depend on provider availability and quotas. You can continue reading lessons when those services are unavailable.
